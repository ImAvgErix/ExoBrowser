## Why Exo Browser

Most browsers make you choose: a blocker you have to set up, a password manager that wants an account, tabs that pile up and a look you can't change. Exo Browser comes with the best of each already on, and nothing to configure.

- **Blocking that's always at its best.** Exo Shield is built in and always on, at its strongest: ads, trackers, malware, cookie notices and pop-ups are blocked before they load, on every site. There is no setting to get wrong and nothing a site can switch off.
- **YouTube and Twitch without ads.** YouTube's ads are blocked like any other. Twitch puts its ads inside the stream itself, so Exo Shield asks for each stream from a country Twitch shows no ads in; the video still comes straight from Twitch, at full quality.
- **Your passwords stay yours.** Exo Vault keeps your logins, cards, notes and identities encrypted on this PC, with no account and no cloud. It fills sign-in forms, makes strong passwords, shows 2FA codes and opens with Windows Hello.
- **Tabs that stay in order.** Spaces for work, school and play, favorites in every space, pinned tabs in folders, live folders that show a site's newest posts, and an archive that tidies away tabs you stopped using.
- **Private by default.** Every address lookup goes encrypted to Cloudflare, Microsoft Edge's balanced tracking prevention is on, private tabs keep nothing, and there is no Exo account, no ads and no telemetry.
- **Made to look at.** Exo Launcher's look: one Bar of Liquid Glass that frosts the page as it scrolls beneath, Inter type, Light, Dark or Void (pure black, for OLED screens), themes in one click and tabs across the top or down the side.
- **Easy to move in.** One click brings your bookmarks, history, sign-ins, extensions and passwords from the other browsers on this PC, and Arc's spaces, favorites and folders from its files.

## Tabs your way

![A new tab in the Work space: your bookmarks as glass cards, your favorites and spaces in the Bar](docs/media/newtab.jpg)

- **Spaces:** each with its own tabs, icon, color and appearance. Alt+1 to 9 switch them, a tab dragged onto one moves there, and a site's links from your other apps can open in the space you pick for it.
- **Favorites and pinned tabs:** favorites show in every space. A pinned tab remembers its page, its icon takes it back when it wanders, and closing it only puts it to sleep.
- **Folders and live folders:** with tabs down the side, pinned tabs sit in folders, folders inside folders too. Right-click a site's tab and choose Follow this site in a live folder: its newest posts show in the folder, checked every 30 minutes.
- **Split view and Peek:** up to four tabs side by side (Ctrl+Shift+=), or Shift+click a link to read it in a card over the page and Ctrl+O to keep it as a tab.
- **Private tabs:** Ctrl+Shift+N opens a tab whose cookies and sign-ins stay apart from your other tabs and go when the last private tab closes. Nothing it visits reaches your history.
- **Ctrl+Tab in the order you used tabs,** as Alt+Tab does with windows: a quick press goes back to the tab before.
- **Archive tabs** (off by default) closes tabs you haven't opened in a while into an archive that Search still finds.
- **Little Exo:** links from your other apps can open in a small window of their own, and Ctrl+O moves one into your tabs.

## Search

![Search over the page, a Wikipedia chip in the field and that site's pages from your history under it](docs/media/search.jpg)

One field for everything: an address opens, anything else goes to your search engine (Brave Search unless you pick another). Suggestions come from your tabs, bookmarks and history on this PC; nothing is sent while you type.

- **Search one site:** type its keyword and a space ("yt cats" searches YouTube), or its address and Tab. It shows as a chip, with that site's pages from your history under it. **Settings > Search** keeps the list and adds your own.
- **Everything at once:** **Ctrl+K** finds a tab, bookmark, page from your history or one of the browser's actions.
- **Copy link:** **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies the address without the tracking parts that share buttons add.

## Make it yours

![Settings > Mods: the Midnight theme, small tweaks and your own CSS](docs/media/mods.jpg)

- **Look:** Light, Dark or Void, Liquid Glass or solid, an accent color, a compact Bar, tab size, text size and reduced motion.
- **Mods:** eight themes that set the look in one click, small tweaks (tab numbers, calm icons, no new tab button, square corners, the accent on the Bar) and your own CSS for Exo Browser itself.
- **Boosts:** right-click a tab to zap things off a site's pages for good, or give a site a style of your own. Boosted pages paint boosted from their first frame, and **Settings > Boosts** lists them, each with a switch.

## Exo Shield

![Settings > Privacy: Exo Shield, always on](docs/media/settings.jpg)

Exo Shield blocks before anything loads, on every site:

- **Ads and trackers:** uBlock Origin Lite's filters with complete filtering everywhere (the extension's strongest mode), plus HaGeZi's Multi PRO list of about 200,000 ad, tracking and malware sites.
- **Annoyances:** cookie notices, overlays, notification prompts, chat and social widgets, and tracking parameters stripped from links.
- **Twitch:** TTV LOL PRO, set to send nothing about you to its developers.
- **Secure DNS:** every lookup over HTTPS to Cloudflare, with no unencrypted fallback.

It is locked on: it has no settings, it can't be turned off or removed, and every start puts its rules back exactly. It runs in private tabs too. Settings > About lists its parts and when its lists were built.

## Exo Vault

![Exo Vault: logins, cards, notes and identities](docs/media/vault.jpg)

A password manager in the browser, with everything kept on this PC:

- **What it keeps:** logins with 2FA codes, cards, secure notes and identities, with favorites, a trash and each login's earlier passwords.
- **Signing in:** a menu on sign-in fields fills your login, and Exo Vault offers to save a new one or update a changed one.
- **Password health:** weak passwords, passwords used on more than one site, and sites that offer 2FA where you haven't set it up, worked out on your PC.
- **Locked when you're away:** after the time you choose, when Windows locks and when the PC sleeps. A copied password leaves the clipboard after 30 seconds and never reaches Windows' clipboard history.
- **Moving in and out:** bring passwords from the browsers on this PC, or from a Proton Pass, Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass, Dashlane, KeePass, Safari or any browser's export. Export everything as one encrypted file to open on another PC.

Nobody can reset your master password, Exo included, so pick one you'll remember.

## How it works

Exo Browser is a native Windows app (C++) built on Microsoft Edge WebView2, the Chromium engine that comes with Windows and that Windows keeps up to date. Pages render exactly as they do in Edge and Chrome, and Chrome Web Store extensions work.

- **Its own interface.** The Bar, the new tab page, Settings and Exo Vault are Exo Browser's own pages, drawn in their own profile with no extensions and no access to the web.
- **Exo Shield** is uBlock Origin Lite with HaGeZi's Multi PRO list and TTV LOL PRO, merged into one built-in extension. Its lists ship with the browser, so it fetches nothing to block.
- **Exo Vault** is one file on your PC, encrypted with AES-256-GCM by Windows. Its key comes from your master password through Argon2id (256 MiB of memory, so guessing is slow), and Windows Hello can open it too.

## Privacy and safety

- **No Exo account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry.** Exo Browser has no server of its own. Your history, bookmarks, spaces, settings and vault stay in `%LocalAppData%\ExoBrowser`.
- **What leaves your PC** is what browsing needs: the sites you open, your address lookups (to Cloudflare), your searches (to the search engine or site you pick), the feeds of sites you follow in live folders, the Twitch playlist requests Exo Shield routes, Chrome Web Store downloads when you add an extension, and the update check to GitHub. The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) lists every destination and what is sent.
- **Your passwords never leave this PC** unless you export them yourself, and an export is encrypted with a password you choose.

## Install

1. Download **[ExoBrowser-Setup.exe](https://github.com/ImAvgErix/ExoBrowser/releases/latest/download/ExoBrowser-Setup.exe)**.
2. Run it and choose Install, then Launch Exo Browser. First run sets up Exo Vault, brings your things over and offers to make Exo Browser your default browser.
3. New versions show in **Settings > About**: **Install update** downloads it, checks it and installs it over the one you have, and your tabs, history, sign-ins and vault stay. After an update, Exo Browser shows what changed.

**Requirements:** Windows 11 x64 with WebView2 (built into Windows 11; Setup installs it if it's missing). Exo Browser installs for your Windows user only, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ExoBrowser\app`, and needs no administrator rights. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time; choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Each release lists the installer's SHA-256 so you can check your download.
